# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: discuss the work, not the person. Disagreement about a design, a measurement or a claim is
expected and welcome - this project's whole method is refusing to accept a claim without evidence,
and that standard applies to maintainers first. Harassment, personal attacks, dismissiveness about
someone's experience level and publishing private information are not welcome.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/17-of-12/fleet-filter/security/advisories/new) if the
matter is sensitive, or by opening an issue if it can be discussed in public. Reports are read by the
maintainers only, and a report about a maintainer goes to the other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently
exclude a participant. The response is proportional to the behaviour and its pattern, and the person
reporting it will be told what happened where that is possible without exposing them.
